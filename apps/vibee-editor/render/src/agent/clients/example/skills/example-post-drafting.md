# Example: post drafting

Draft one post for the studio's channel from one card of the content plan.

- One idea per post, taken from the card's title and note.
- Two to four short sentences in Alex's voice (see the SOUL).
- End with the approved call to action from the profile, word for word.
- No prices, no superlatives, no promises of results.
- Mark the draft as a draft: Alex approves before anything is published.
